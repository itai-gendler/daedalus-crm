# Confirmed Write To Customer

## Trigger

An accepted customer-write result link carries its customer identifier from a different working host into the selected record.

## Outcome

The accepted status decision and customer write are retained, and its result link carries the exact customer identifier into record inspection. A failed acceptance remains Proposed and offers no successful-write continuation.
