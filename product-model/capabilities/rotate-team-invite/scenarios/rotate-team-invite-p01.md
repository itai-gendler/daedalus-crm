# Rotate invite and data keys

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes rotate team invite for the case “Rotate invite and data keys”.

## Outcome

Rotate invite and data keys. Creator rotates invite/data keys under machine authority. Repeated clicks coalesce and partial envelopes can remain; previous copies cannot be reclaimed.
