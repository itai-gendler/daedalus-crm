# Winning stage hands to registration

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes advance opportunity for the case “Winning stage hands to registration”.

## Outcome

Winning stage hands to registration. Stage movement is distinct from guarded status normalization and can leave OPEN at the winning stage. Entering the winning stage opens the template-specific financial process.
