# Customer/owner unavailable

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes create opportunity for the case “Customer/owner unavailable”.

## Outcome

A missing customer or an unavailable selected owner prevents the requested opportunity from being reported saved. The request does not silently replace either association with another customer or owner.
