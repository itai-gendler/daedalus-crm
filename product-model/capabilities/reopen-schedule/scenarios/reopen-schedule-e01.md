# Missing target/task completion time cleared

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes reopen schedule for the case “Missing target/task completion time cleared”.

## Outcome

A missing plan/task reports its target error. A reopened task clears Completion time; a reopened plan has no separate completion-time fact to clear.
