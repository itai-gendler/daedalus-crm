# Mixed targets/concurrency/transfer failure

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes assign owner for the case “Mixed targets/concurrency/transfer failure”.

## Outcome

The result distinguishes updated, already assigned and missing customers. Customer ownership changes precede unfinished-work transfer, so transfer failure can leave the new customer owner in place without a completed carry-over result; completed historical work remains with its recorded owner.
