# Guarded linked-order rejection

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes reopen opportunity for the case “Guarded linked-order rejection”.

## Outcome

The linked-order guard refuses the reopen that would violate that order's established constraint; it does not remove the order to make reopening succeed.
