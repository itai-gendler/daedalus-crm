# Plan apply conflict/duplicate/dependency outcome

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes accept draft for the case “Plan apply conflict/duplicate/dependency outcome”.

## Outcome

Repeated same-content plan confirmation within ten minutes is refused. A failed ordinary plan save releases the reservation and keeps the card Proposed. It does not force a replacement plan past the underlying action’s refusal or create an undo receipt for this kind.
