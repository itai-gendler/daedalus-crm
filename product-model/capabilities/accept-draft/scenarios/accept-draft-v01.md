# Refuse invalid/inaccessible status acceptance

## Trigger

A CRM member invokes accept draft for the case “Refuse invalid/inaccessible status acceptance”.

## Outcome

The status draft fails its kind/field, current-target, comparison-snapshot or named-member validation. The acceptance error leaves the draft Proposed and performs no successful target write; a failed content reservation is released.
